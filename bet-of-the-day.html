How the call is made

Waiting for a book to misprice a line is one way to find value. This page also makes its own number. It rates every NHL team from game results since October 2024, with an Elo model that credits margin of victory, home ice and a pull back toward average each new season. It then compares its win probability to the eight-book market consensus.

  1. Strong bet. Blended EV of 2% or more. Quarter Kelly, capped at 2% of bankroll.
  2. Model pick. Model-only EV of 3% or more. One-eighth Kelly, capped at 1%.
  3. No call. Neither clears.

The model scores a log loss of 0.680 over 2,897 games, against 0.690 for always picking the home rate. The settings were tuned on the same games, so the true figure is slightly worse. It beats a naive baseline. It has not been shown to beat the market, and the ledger below is how that gets tested. The model's weight in the blend only goes up if the record earns it.
