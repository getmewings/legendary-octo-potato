No method makes betting reliable

Sportsbooks build a margin into every price and the sharpest ones are right far more often than any model you or I can build. The goal is small, repeatable, positive expected value, sized so a losing streak cannot end the bankroll. A bet of the day is a candidate with a measured edge, never a promise of a win.

How the pick is made

  1. Fair price. Take a sharp market (Pinnacle, Circa, Bookmaker). Remove the vig by dividing each implied probability by their sum.
  2. Line shop. Compare that fair probability to the best price across soft books.
  3. Edge. Bet only if EV is at least 2%.
  4. Stake. Quarter Kelly, capped at 2% of bankroll.
  5. Prove it. Track closing line value. If your bets beat the closing price over hundreds of wagers, the process works, whatever the short-term record says.
